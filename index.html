FAQ

Common questions.

How many prompts are included?

The current product materials describe more than 400 prompts organised across 12 business categories and 11 professional role groupings.

Does RealtyPrompt Pro generate AI responses itself?

No. RealtyPrompt Pro is a desktop prompt-library and workflow tool. You prepare and copy prompts for use with the AI service you choose.

Does it require a subscription?

The planned commercial model is a one-time A$149 purchase rather than a recurring RealtyPrompt Pro subscription.

Will it work offline?

Normal application use is intended to remain offline-capable after the required licence activation. Using an online third-party AI service will, of course, require connectivity to that service.

How many devices can use one licence?

The planned first-release licence permits up to three active devices. Production licensing and release remain in preparation.

Does the software replace legal or professional review?

No. Prompt content and AI-generated outputs must be reviewed for accuracy, suitability and applicable legal or regulatory requirements before professional use.

RealtyPrompt Pro is preparing for commercial release.

This public site describes the current product direction without enabling a production checkout before licensing and release gates are complete.
